# Appendix A. Glossary

| Term | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AED | Annotation edit distance, a 0 to 1 score of how well a gene model agrees with its evidence, used by MAKER. | `README.md (MAKER2 entry and the AED idea entry in section 15)` |
| Ab initio prediction | Gene finding from the genome sequence with a statistical model, without aligned evidence. | [doi:10.1186/s12864-020-6707-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-020-6707-9) |
| BUSCO | Benchmarking Universal Single-Copy Orthologs; reports complete (C), duplicated (D), fragmented (F) and missing (M) conserved genes for a lineage. | [doi:10.1093/molbev/msab199](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msab199) |
| CDS | Coding sequence: the part of a transcript translated into protein. | [doi:10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44) |
| Chimeric gene model | Two or more adjacent genes wrongly fused into one model. | [doi:10.1186/s12864-025-11765-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-025-11765-w) |
| EVM | EVidenceModeler: builds a weighted consensus gene set from several evidence sources. | [doi:10.1186/gb-2008-9-1-r7](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2008-9-1-r7) |
| GFF3 | Tab-separated format for genomic features; feature types follow the Sequence Ontology. | [doi:10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44) |
| Haplotig | A contig that is a second copy of a region from the other haplotype. | [doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa025](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa025) |
| Isoform | One of several transcripts from the same gene that differ in exons or ends. | [doi:10.1038/nmeth.2714](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.2714) |
| Lift-over | Mapping gene models from an annotated assembly onto another assembly. | [doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa1016](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa1016) |
| Soft-masking | Writing repeat bases in lower case so predictors can down-weight them without deleting sequence. | `examples/plant_sim/01_vitis_s1_s7/plan.md` |
| Sequence Ontology (SO) | Controlled vocabulary for the parts of a genomic annotation. | [doi:10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44) |
| TE | Transposable element; assemblies of large plant genomes now span TEs, which make up much of their repeats. | [doi:10.1186/s13059-019-1905-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1905-y) |
| UTR | Untranslated region at the 5 prime or 3 prime end of an mRNA. | [doi:10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2005-6-5-r44) |
