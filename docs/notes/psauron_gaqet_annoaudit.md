# PSAURON · GAQET2 · AnnoAudit (QC packs)

- PSAURON: https://github.com/salzberg-lab/PSAURON — ML score for “does this CDS look real?” (already in our qualify spine).  
- GAQET2: https://github.com/victorgcb1987/GAQET2 — bundles AGAT+BUSCO+OMArk+PSAURON(+…) for annotation audit.  
- AnnoAudit: https://github.com/ERGA-consortium/AnnoAudit — ERGA Nextflow audit (stats, RNA support, BUSCO, OMArk, BRH).  
- Also: AGAT (https://github.com/NBISweden/AGAT), gffcompare (https://github.com/gpertea/gffcompare).  
- **Our mapping:** optional one-stop QC wrappers around our checklist; keep individual metrics in METHODS.
